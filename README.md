# Computer Vision Projects

A portfolio of five computer vision projects focused on deep learning, medical imaging, and robotics.

## 01. Explainable AI for Object Recognition
**Status:** Published

ResNet-18 image classification on CIFAR-10, with Grad-CAM visualisations.

## 02. Fruit Quality Inspection
**Status:** Published

Deep-learning-based fruit image classification and quality inspection.

## 03. Medical Image Classification and Analysis
**Status:** On Hold

Planned medical AI research involving image classification, analysis, and explainability.

## 04. Waste Sorting Robot
**Status:** In Development

A computer vision system for waste detection and classification, with the goal of automated robotic sorting.

## 05. Gesture Recognition for Robot Control
**Status:** Planned

A vision-based system to recognise hand gestures and map them to robotic control commands.

## Technologies

Python, PyTorch, OpenCV, deep learning, and computer vision.

## Disclaimer

Medical and robotic AI projects are research and educational work, not validated clinical or autonomous surgical systems.
